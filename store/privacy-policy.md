# Privacy Policy – Drip: Water Reminder Buddy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Drip is a browser extension that reminds you to drink water. This policy explains what data Drip handles.

## What Drip stores

Drip stores only the following, and only inside your own Chrome browser:

- Your settings (reminder interval, snooze length, animation, character size, sound, active hours, daily goal).
- A daily count of how many glasses of water you logged (kept for the last 60 days).
- The time of your next reminder.

Settings are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so if you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google syncs them between your own devices. Glass counts and timer state are saved with `chrome.storage.local` and stay on the device.

## What Drip does not do

- Drip does not collect, read, store or transmit the content of the web pages you visit.
- Drip does not track your browsing history.
- Drip has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no remote servers. It makes no network requests.
- Drip does not sell or share any data with anyone.

## Why Drip needs access to websites

To show the animated reminder on top of the page you are using, Drip inserts its character into the active tab at the moment a reminder is due. It does not read the page. The "idle" permission is used only to check whether you are away from the computer, so reminders wait until you are back.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Drip deletes all data it stored. You can also reset your settings and today's count from the Drip settings page.
